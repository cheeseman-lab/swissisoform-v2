Does every factual claim in this synthesis trace to the category reads or the
criteria evidence provided, without importing outside knowledge about this gene?

Judge on substance, NOT style or length. Named interactors, residue ranges,
promoters, fold-changes and mechanisms are only merits if they appear in the
inputs. Drawn from memory of the gene, they are defects — the synthesis is meant
to read THIS evidence, not recall literature.

An inference clearly marked as hypothesis is acceptable. The same inference stated
as established fact is not.

Score 1: Makes central claims found nowhere in the inputs, or cites numbers absent from them.
Score 2: Adds unsupported specifics -- mechanisms, interactors, magnitudes or positions not present in the inputs.
Score 3: Largely grounded, but states as established what the inputs only suggest.
Score 4: Grounded throughout, with an occasional inference not flagged as one.
Score 5: Every claim traces to the inputs, and inference beyond them is explicitly marked as hypothesis.
