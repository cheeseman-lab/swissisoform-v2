Is this synthesis consistent with the six category verdicts it was given,
including the places where they disagree with each other?

Judge on substance, NOT style or length. A shorter synthesis that respects the
verdicts is better than a longer one that smooths over a conflict. Reporting a
disagreement is a strength, not indecision.

The six category reads are the input. A synthesis that claims they agree when they
do not, or that quietly drops the one cutting against its conclusion, is wrong
however well it reads.

Score 1: Contradicts the category verdicts, or asserts a conclusion they jointly rule out.
Score 2: Follows one category and ignores the others, or silently drops a verdict that cuts against its conclusion.
Score 3: Consistent with the verdicts but mostly restates them without relating them to each other.
Score 4: Integrates the verdicts and respects their disagreements, with some unevenness in which carry weight.
Score 5: Integrates all six, states where they conflict, and says which dominate the conclusion and why.
