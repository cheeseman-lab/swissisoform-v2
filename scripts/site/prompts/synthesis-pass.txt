You are writing the whole-isoform biological judgment for one alt-TIS isoform (extension, truncation, or uORF/altORF) in SwissIsoform v2.

Your job is ADDITIVE, not a summary. Given what this GENE/protein is already known to do, say how THIS isoform changes or diverges from that established function or character. Do NOT restate the category verdicts or walk the six categories one by one — that is a laundry list and is explicitly not wanted. Start from the known function; end at "so this isoform does X to it."

Input:
- `gene` — the protein's ESTABLISHED identity: `function` (a literature-grounded description of what it does), `keywords` (its functional/pathway terms), `subcellular_location`. This is your BASELINE — the thing the isoform diverges from. Trust it; it is what the reader already knows about the gene.
- The isoform's identity (gene, transcript, ORF type, lengths, differential sequence, and `differential_region_location` — where the differential region sits; trust it verbatim).
- `category_reads` — one `{verdict, reasoning}` per CDLMPS category (Conservation, Detection, Localization, Mutation Landscape, Predicted Structure, Structural Characteristics). Evidence you draw on, NOT the framing to reproduce.
- `criteria_evidence` — the raw per-criterion numbers behind those verdicts (all 15 criteria).
- coarse existence/functional headline scores — a consistency check, not the framing.

The 15 criteria behind the category reads:
- C Conservation: C1 primate AA-identity, C2 mammalian AA-identity, C3 absolute phyloP constraint over the unique region.
- D Detection: D1 multi-cell-line reproducibility, D2 initiation efficiency, D3 unique-peptide mass spec.
- L Localization: L1 DeepLoc compartment/signal change, L2 SignalP/TargetP targeting change.
- M Mutation Landscape: M1 germline tolerance/constraint (gnomAD depletion + ESM-C — NOT disease burden), M2 disease-variant density enrichment (ClinVar/COSMIC).
- P Predicted Structure: P1 unique-region folding (pLDDT only), P2 shared-region core-fold perturbation (Cα RMSD, only trusted when pTM/pLDDT are high).
- S Structural Characteristics: S1 real-InterPro-domain gain/loss, S2 whole-protein biophysical shift, S3 differential ESM-C SAE features (magnitude check on the strongest shared-feature activation shift; provisional labels).

Guardrails:
  - The reader is already ON the isoform page: gene, ORF type, both lengths, and the differential sequence are displayed above your text. Do NOT restate that identity — lead straight with the finding.
  - Differential-region directionality: alt-TIS isoforms differ ONLY at the N-terminus — an extension ADDS N-terminal sequence, a truncation REMOVES N-terminal sequence; the C-terminus and stop codon are invariant. Never describe the differential region as C-terminal.
  - germline tolerance/constraint (gnomAD depletion ratio < 1× = important) is NOT pathogenic burden; disease-variant density is a separate signal.
  - Introduce no fact the input does not state — never extrapolate biology (e.g. organelle retargeting) the reads do not report. If the evidence does not support a functional change, say so plainly and set low confidence.

Output JSON only (no code fences), fields:

  - "headline": one sentence (≤160 chars) — the single dominant call: how this isoform changes the gene's function. States the finding, not the identity.
  - "divergence_hypothesis": 1–3 sentences — the claim: how this isoform modifies or diverges from the gene's established function or character. Lead with it. HTML-safe Markdown (**bold**, *italic*, `code`); no links/images.

Controlled tag vocabulary (assign only the ones the evidence supports; each is one axis of functional change):
  - "Relocalization" — DeepLoc predicts a different subcellular compartment for the isoform vs canonical.
  - "Targeting-signal change" — a SignalP/TargetP signal or targeting peptide is gained or lost.
  - "Domain loss" — a real InterPro domain is lost in the differential region (typically a truncation).
  - "Domain gain" — a real InterPro domain is gained in the differential region (typically an extension).
  - "Truncated functional region" — a truncation removes an N-terminal region that is structured, conserved, or domain-bearing (functionally important, not just sequence).
  - "Structured N-terminal extension" — an extension adds a confidently-folded N-terminal region (high differential-region pLDDT).
  - "Disorder shift" — a whole-protein biophysical shift in disorder/charge/hydropathy (S2 fired).
  - "Core refold" — the retained (shared) region is predicted to fold differently, AND the structure confidence supports it (pTM and shared-region pLDDT high). Do NOT assign on RMSD alone when confidence is low.
  - "Conservation-backed" — the unique region is conserved across primates/mammals (high AA-identity or strong phyloP).
  - "Disease-enriched" — ClinVar/COSMIC disease variants concentrate in the unique region.
  - "Germline-constrained" — the unique region is under germline constraint (gnomAD depletion and/or ESM-C constraint). Valid on truncations; on extensions the unique region was non-coding, so this is usually NOT supported.
  - "Mass-spec supported" — a validated isoform-unique peptide confirms the isoform is translated.
  - "Multi-cell-line reproducible" — the isoform is detected across multiple cell lines.
  - "Interpretable-feature shift" — differential ESM-C SAE features fire (S3 scored true).
