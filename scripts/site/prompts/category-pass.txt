Isoform KIND decides which metrics are even valid — read `orf_type` first and apply the matching rule. Do not re-derive these; they are properties of the annotation, not findings about this isoform:
- EXTENSION (`extended`): the unique region is sequence that was 5'UTR or intron in the canonical annotation — it was never coding. BOTH germline-constraint inputs are therefore uninformative about protein constraint here: the gnomAD depletion ratio measures variation in never-coding nucleotides (shaped by UTR/splicing selection and by coverage differences, not by this protein), and the ESM-C score is out-of-distribution, reflecting sequence composition and local predictability rather than evolutionary intolerance. Expect zero strongly-constrained ESM-C positions in the unique region — that is the NORM for extensions, not a negative finding. Never present the two as conflicting, never adjudicate between them, and never report their disagreement as a result: neither is measuring what it appears to measure, so their disagreement carries no information.
- TRUNCATION (`truncated`): the removed region IS canonical coding sequence. Both germline-constraint inputs are valid here, and a genuine disagreement between them IS informative and worth stating.
- SEPARATE ORF (`uorf`, `uoorf`, `internal_oof`, `3utr_orf`, `alt_orf`): the entire isoform is the differential region — there is no shared region at all. The gnomAD depletion ratio is structurally undefined (its denominator is the shared region) and the shared-core fold RMSD is not evaluable. Treat these as absent by construction, not as missing data.

Directionality — get these right:
- The two germline-constraint signals are INDEPENDENT, either-or evidence, not two measurements of one quantity, and they run in OPPOSITE numerical directions for the same conclusion (gnomAD: lower = constrained; ESM-C constraint_delta: higher = constrained). The scorer accepts either one alone. Do not expect them to corroborate, and do not treat one being quiet as undercutting the other.
- The differential region is always N-terminal — the alt start codon moves, so the change is at the N-terminus; the C-terminus and stop codon are invariant. Never describe the region (or the sequence it adds/loses) as C-terminal, even when it is acidic or otherwise looks like a tail.
- M1 is germline TOLERANCE/CONSTRAINT: a gnomAD depletion ratio < 1 means population variation AVOIDS the unique region (evidence it is important); a positive ESM-C constraint_delta means the model predicts the unique region's residues better than the shared core, i.e. finds them more conserved (it is mean logP(wt) unique minus shared). Never read gnomAD variants as disease/pathogenic burden.
- M2 is DISEASE density: a ClinVar/COSMIC enrichment ratio >= 1 means disease variants CONCENTRATE in the unique region.
- P1 is folding only (unique-region pLDDT ≥ threshold); the biophysical signal now lives in S2 (whole-protein gravy/charge/disorder |delta|, isoform vs canonical). S1 counts only real InterPro domains (not disorder/coiled-coil/low-complexity).
- P2 is a shared-region core-fold change (Cα RMSD) and is only meaningful when the shared region is confidently folded in BOTH structures — the reason string carries the min shared-region pLDDT. Never call an elevated RMSD a real refold when that confidence is low; a floppy, low-pLDDT loop moves the backbone without any conformational meaning. Weigh the reported RMSD against its pLDDT before treating it as signal.
- C1/C2 are amino-acid identity across primates/mammals; C3 is absolute phyloP (|mean| >= ~2 = strong constraint).
- S3 (sae) scores on MAGNITUDE, not presence: the strongest shared-feature activation shift must exceed a threshold. Gained/lost feature counts are context only — two proteins of different length always differ in hundreds of features, so a large count is not evidence of anything. Its feature labels are auto-generated and provisional, not curated annotation: many carry no content at all (hollow ones are withheld, leaving only a feature index), and many others merely describe the feature's own activation pattern or restate sequence composition that S2 already scores in the same category. Never name, quote or interpret an SAE feature label in the reasoning, and never let a label move the verdict — cite the activation-shift magnitude, not the labels. Never invent biology the inputs do not state.
